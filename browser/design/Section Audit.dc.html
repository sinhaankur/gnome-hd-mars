Mars HAWC · Section audit26 November 2026

What each screen has, what's broken, what comes next

A pass through the browser edition, screen by screen. "Built" means it's in the current build. "Add next" lists proposals, ordered by value, and the batch at the end is my recommended build order.

01 · Main menu

The globe

Built
  • Mars from MOLA and HiRISE, Phobos, Deimos and the Ares Ring
  • The real sky for today: Milky Way, 9,096 catalogued stars, the Sun and planets at their true positions
  • 24 stations, each named for the first probe or rover to land there
  • Orbit trails that fade behind each body
  • When the menu is left alone for 14 s, the camera drifts to the night side so the Sun rises over the limb
Gaps and bugs
  • Labels collide where sites sit close together, for example Viking, Sojourner and ExoMars
  • The Sun and planets only show from the night side, which is correct physics but easy to miss
  • No ambient sound bed
Add next
  • Label decluttering: place labels by priority and add leader lines
  • A hover card for each city: probe, landing year, ice depth, owner
  • A low ambient score under the menu
02 · Intro

Opening

Built
  • Narrated text cards with radio voice
  • A skip button
Gaps and bugs
  • There are no shots yet, only text over the globe
Add next
  • A storyboarded 60-second opening (EST, FULL, CU): the Accord, the first Darken landing, the Founder's tanker
03 · Agency select

Choosing a flag

Built
  • 7 agency cards with flag, stations and ore a sol
Gaps and bugs
  • Agencies differ only by the stations they hold. The brief calls for asymmetric factions
Add next
  • One doctrine perk and one HAWC variant per agency
  • A commander line for each card
04 · War room

Campaign

Built
  • Nine operation types, from defend through to orbital assault
  • Briefings with ice and cell yield
  • Armory Mk I–III, orbital strike and supply drop
  • Red Feed
  • Sol economy in ore and cells
Gaps and bugs
  • The supply chain is invisible: no convoys or landers appear on the map
  • No population, control towers or settlement tiers
Add next
  • Animated convoy routes and lander arcs on the globe
  • Settlement tier badges (outpost, base, town, city)
  • A preview of each operation's effect on cell stock
05 · Training

Scripted missions

Built
  • Mission list with briefs, including Level 1, Take the mining base
Gaps and bugs
  • Nothing teaches the hijack loop in isolation
Add next
  • A two-minute hijack drill: eject, GASHR, board, steal
06 · Hangar

Machines

Built
  • The Striker and 4 enemy units, each with its own animations
  • 3 liveries
  • Teardown x-ray: seated pilot, cell bay, SOFC stack, actuators, all with callouts
Gaps and bugs
  • Fixed this pass: the pilot was unlit and hard to see, and callouts ran off screen
  • Only the bipedal class exists
Add next
  • Field manual tab: every component, its job and its power source
  • Tank, hovercraft and walker hulls
  • A damage-state preview
07 · Fuel and arm

Before launch

Built
  • Cells, cannon rounds, missiles and ore cost
Gaps and bugs
  • It doesn't show where the cells come from
Add next
  • Loadout presets
  • A cell-source line, for example "Phoenix glacier, 3 cells a sol"
08 · Mission

Play

Built
  • Chase, first-person and cinematic views
  • Pilot on foot with jetpack and GASHR hijack
  • Cells that drain and can be swapped, heat, night vision
  • Day/night cycle with moonlight, clouds, dust devils and wind
  • Explosions and progressive damage
  • Heritage probes at their real sites, MOLA horizon
  • Film look, procedural audio, kill cam, points
Gaps and bugs
  • The whole interface re-renders 12 times a second for the HUD. This is the most likely cause of the hitching
  • Micro-animations are off-tone: the score scales up 1.4× and pickups pop with an overshoot
  • No trauma shake or hit-stop
  • Only one hijackable class
Add next
  • Isolate the HUD so the rest of the interface stops re-rendering
  • One motion language, picked from the motion study
  • Trauma shake and hit-stop, plus a field log and scanner (see the research report)
  • Rover and lander traffic
09 · Pause, debrief, settings

Around the mission

Built
  • Pause menu
  • Debrief: points, time, kills, accuracy, HAWCs taken, campaign news
  • Settings: difficulty, mouse, invert Y, graphics, day length
Gaps and bugs
  • No controller support, which Steam Deck Verified requires
  • No shake slider and no subtitles toggle
Add next
  • Controller input with button glyphs
  • Shake slider 0–100%, subtitles, key rebinding
Recommended next batch
  1. Snappiness. Isolate the HUD from the rest of the interface, then profile a mission on a mid-range laptop.
  2. One motion language. Pick a direction in the motion study and apply it to every button, panel, pickup and transition.
  3. Controller support, with glyphs, a shake slider and subtitles.
  4. Asset kit: city pieces, mining machines, haul trucks, and cell and mineral containers.
  5. Supply-chain traffic running on the globe and in missions.
