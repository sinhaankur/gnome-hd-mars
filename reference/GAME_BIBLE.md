# G-NOME Game Bible (extracted from disc `LABTEXT.DAT` + research)

## Levels / Campaign
- **20 single-player campaign missions**, organized into **4 campaigns** (one per faction territory):
  1. **Darken Republic** — Gant crosses Darken territory to reach Kylie.
  2. **Bendian Merc** territory.
  3. **Scorp** territory — defended by Scorp Imperial Warriors; ends at the laboratory.
  4. **Shalten Frontier** — pursuing the G-Nome and its captors.
- Plus a separate set of **training tutorials**.
- Missions are multi-objective (some require exiting the HAWC on foot to infiltrate buildings).

## Factions
Darken · Union (player side) · Bendian Mercs · Scorp (alien empire). Merc units have **no auto-eject**.
